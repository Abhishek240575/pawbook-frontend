Delhi / NCR Region Advisory
Delhi's high dog population density and monsoon season increase risk of Leptospirosis (via contaminated water), Canine Influenza (dog parks, kennels), and tick-borne diseases. PawBook recommends quarterly deworming and monthly tick/flea prevention for Labradors in Delhi NCR.
