Plan your first trip with your pet!

📋

Visit the Vet First

Get a health certificate and ensure vaccinations are up to date at least 10 days before travel.

🎒

Pack a Pet Travel Kit

Bring food, water, bowls, leash, waste bags, medications, and a first aid kit for your pet.

🚗

Car Safety

Use a pet carrier or seat belt harness. Never let pets ride with their heads out the window.

🏨

Book Pet-Friendly Hotels

Always confirm pet policies before booking. Many hotels in India now welcome pets with a deposit.

✈️

Flying with Pets

Check airline pet policies. Most Indian airlines allow pets in cabin (under 7 kg) or cargo. Book early.

🌡️

Watch the Weather

Avoid travelling to hot destinations in summer with brachycephalic breeds (Pugs, Bulldogs, Persians).

🏷️

ID Tags & Microchip

Ensure your pet has a current ID tag and is microchipped — essential if they get lost during travel.
