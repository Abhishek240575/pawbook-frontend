📋

Book Well in Advance

Airlines and trains limit the number of pets per journey. Book at least 48–72 hours in advance for domestic travel, and 2–4 weeks for international. Some airlines only allow 1–2 pets per flight.

🩺

Pre-Travel Health Check

Get a fitness-to-travel certificate from a licensed veterinarian within 10 days of travel. Many carriers and countries require this. Pets with respiratory or cardiac conditions may not be cleared for air travel.

🏷️

Identification & Microchip

All pets must have an ID tag with owner name and phone number. International travel requires ISO 11784/11785 microchip (15-digit). India recommends microchipping under Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act.

📦

Approved Carrier / Crate

IATA-approved carriers are mandatory for air travel. Crate must be large enough for pet to stand, turn, and lie down. Hard-sided crates required for cargo; soft-sided allowed for cabin on some airlines.

🌡️

Temperature Restrictions

Many airlines restrict pet cargo travel when outside temperatures exceed 29°C or fall below 7°C. Snub-nosed (brachycephalic) breeds like Pugs, Bulldogs, and Persians face additional restrictions due to breathing risks.

💊

Sedation Policy

IATA and most airlines strongly advise AGAINST sedating pets for travel as sedation can cause respiratory and cardiovascular issues at altitude. If medically necessary, consult your vet and inform the airline in advance.

⚠️
Brachycephalic (Snub-nosed) Breed Warning

Breeds including Pug, French Bulldog, English Bulldog, Boston Terrier, Shih Tzu, Persian Cat, Himalayan Cat, and Pekingese face higher risk of heat stroke and breathing distress during air travel. Many airlines ban these breeds in cargo. Always confirm with the airline before booking.

Required Travel Documents
Documents required vary by pet type, travel mode, and destination. Keep originals and digital copies of all documents.

🇮🇳 Domestic Travel — India (All Modes)

Vaccination Certificate REQUIRED
Issued by a registered veterinarian. Must show Rabies, Distemper, Parvovirus, and Hepatitis vaccines. Valid within 1 year for domestic travel. Rabies vaccine required at least 30 days before travel.
Health / Fitness-to-Travel Certificate REQUIRED
Issued by a licensed vet within 10 days of travel. Confirms the pet is free from contagious diseases and fit to travel. Required by all airlines; strongly recommended for trains.
Pet Ownership Proof REQUIRED
Purchase receipt, adoption certificate, or municipal pet registration document. Proves you are the rightful owner.
Microchip Documentation RECOMMENDED
Certificate from the vet showing ISO microchip number. Not mandatory for domestic travel but strongly recommended for identification if the pet is lost.
Recent Pet Photo RECOMMENDED
Clear, recent photo of your pet showing coat colour and markings. Useful in case of loss or theft during travel.

🌍 International Travel Documents

⚠️
Start the process at least 3–6 months before travel

Many countries have quarantine rules, blood titre tests, and waiting periods that require months of advance planning. Always check the destination country's official government import rules.

International Health Certificate REQUIRED
Issued by a government-accredited veterinarian and endorsed by the Department of Animal Husbandry (India). Must be done within 10 days of travel. Many countries have their own specific form.
Rabies Vaccination Certificate REQUIRED
Must be valid at time of entry. Some countries (Australia, UK, Japan) require rabies titre test 3–6 months before entry and a mandatory waiting period after vaccination.
ISO Microchip (15-digit) REQUIRED
ISO 11784/11785 compliant 15-digit microchip is mandatory for international travel. Must be implanted BEFORE rabies vaccination to be valid. Confirm chip is readable with a compatible scanner.
Rabies Titre Test (FAV/FAVN) REQUIRED for many countries
Blood test confirming adequate rabies antibody levels (≥0.5 IU/ml). Required by Australia, UK, New Zealand, Japan, Singapore, and others. Must be done at an approved lab. Allow 3–6 months for the waiting period.
Parasite Treatment Certificate REQUIRED for some countries
Treatment for tapeworm, ticks, and fleas administered by a vet within 24–120 hours before entry. Required by EU, UK, Australia, and many others. Specific product names and dosages may be specified.
Import Permit REQUIRED for some countries
Prior approval from the destination country's agriculture/veterinary authority. Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, and many others require an import permit obtained months in advance.
CITES Permit (Exotic Species) REQUIRED for exotic pets
Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species permit for certain birds, reptiles, and exotic animals. Apply through the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (India) or equivalent authority.

Air Travel with Pets
How to book, what to expect, airline-specific policies, and tips for a stress-free flight with your pet.
⚠️
Book the pet SEPARATELY from your ticket

Pet reservations are not automatic. After booking your flight, call the airline's cargo or reservations desk to register your pet. Each airline limits the number of pets per flight (usually 2–4 in cabin, 10–12 in cargo).

📋 How to Book a Flight for Your Pet — Step by Step

1
Check airline's pet policyVisit the airline website or call their cargo helpdesk. Confirm: pet size limits, breed restrictions, temperature embargo dates, cabin vs. cargo options, and fees.
2
Book your own ticket firstGet your PNR/booking reference. You need this to register your pet on the same flight.
3
Call airline's reservations/cargo deskProvide your PNR, pet details (species, breed, age, weight with carrier), and request pet booking. Confirm space availability — it's first-come, first-served.
4
Get an IATA-approved carrierPurchase or rent a carrier that meets IATA Live Animals Regulations. Hard-sided for cargo; soft-sided may be allowed in cabin. Crate must be ventilated on 3 sides, lockable, and leak-proof.
5
Visit vet for health certificateGet the fitness-to-travel certificate from a licensed vet within 10 days of departure. Bring it on the day of travel.
6
Arrive early at the airportArrive 2–3 hours before departure for domestic, 3–4 hours for international. For cargo pets, check-in at the airline's cargo terminal separately (not the main terminal).
7
At check-inPresent your ticket, pet health certificate, vaccination record, and ownership proof. Pay the pet surcharge (collected at check-in). For cabin pets, the carrier goes through the X-ray scanner — take the pet out briefly during screening.
8
After landingFor cargo pets, collect from the airline's baggage claim or cargo area. Present your collection receipt and ID. Inspect your pet immediately for any distress, injury, or disorientation.

IATA-Approved Carrier Requirements

📐

Size Rule

Pet must be able to stand, turn around, and lie down in a natural position. Measure pet standing height (top of head to floor) and length (tip of nose to base of tail) + 10 cm clearance each.

🔒

Secure Latching

Crate must have secure, locking bolts on all 4 sides of the door. Spring latches alone are not acceptable. Zip ties or additional security ties are recommended as backup.

🌬️

Ventilation

Minimum 16% of total wall area must be ventilation. Must have ventilation on at least 3 sides. Ventilation holes should be inward-facing rims to prevent other cargo from blocking airflow.

💧

Food & Water

Attach a water bowl (tip-proof) accessible from outside crate. For journeys over 4 hours, include a food container. Tape a small bag of dry food to the top of the crate with feeding instructions.

📌
Label Requirements on the Crate
  • Owner name, address, phone number (at origin and destination)
  • "LIVE ANIMAL" label in large letters on top and at least one side
  • Directional arrows showing "this side up"
  • Pet's name, species, breed, age, gender, weight
  • Emergency contact number
Train Travel with Pets
Indian Railways allows pets in specific classes. Here's everything you need to know to travel by train with your pet in India.
✅
Indian Railways — Pets Are Permitted

Indian Railways allows dogs, cats, and other animals in the Luggage Van (SLR/brake van) of most trains. Dogs are allowed in First Class AC (1AC) compartments under specific conditions. No pets are allowed in sleeper, 2AC, 3AC, or general coaches.

🚆 How to Book a Train Ticket for Your Pet

1
Book your own ticket firstBook on IRCTC or at the station. For pets in the brake van, you do NOT need to be in the same train — but it is strongly recommended to travel on the same train so you can check on your pet at stops.
2
Visit the station's Parcel OfficeGo to the station's Parcel/Luggage Office (not the main booking counter). Present your pet in a carrier and declare it as a live animal consignment. This is done on the day of travel; advance booking for the pet is not available online.
3
Fill the Live Animals Consignment FormProvide pet details: species, breed, weight, number of animals, carrier dimensions, and destination. Sign the declaration that the animal is healthy and fit to travel.
4
Pay the charges at the Parcel OfficeCharges are based on weight of pet + carrier combined. Minimum charge applies. Pay and collect the consignment note (receipt) — keep this for collecting the pet at destination.
5
Pet is loaded into the Luggage Van (SLR)The guard/parcel staff will place the carrier in the designated area. Ensure the crate is labelled with your contact details and destination. Provide water and food for the journey.
6
At the destinationGo directly to the Parcel Office at the destination station. Present your consignment note and ID. Inspect the pet before leaving the station premises.

🐕 Dogs in First Class AC (1AC) — Special Provision

📌
Only in 1AC Compartment — by Booking the Entire Coupe

A passenger may keep their dog in a 1AC compartment if they book all 4 berths of the coupe exclusively. The dog must be kept under control. This is a special provision under Indian Railways rule and may not apply to all trains.

Book all 4 berths of the 1AC coupe
Must be done at the station or via IRCTC. Inform the booking clerk at the time of reservation that the coupe is being booked for a pet.
Carry health/vaccination certificate
The TTE (Ticket Examiner) may ask for vaccination proof. Carry original documents.
Dog must be on leash at all times
The dog should not enter corridors or other compartments. Owner is responsible for behaviour and cleanliness.
Bus Travel with Pets
Bus travel policies for pets in India vary significantly by operator. Most state-run buses do not allow pets inside the passenger cabin.
⚠️
Most Government Buses Do Not Allow Pets in the Cabin

KSRTC, MSRTC, DTC, and most state transport undertakings do not permit pets inside the passenger compartment. Small pets in carriers may be allowed at the driver's discretion, but this is not guaranteed or official policy.

🚌 Private Bus & Road Travel Options

🚐

Pet-Friendly Private Buses

Some private overnight bus operators (Redbus-listed operators) allow small pets in carriers. Always confirm in advance when booking. Call the operator directly — the online booking system may not reflect the pet policy.

🚗

Self-Drive / Personal Vehicle

Best option for pets. Use a crate, pet seat belt harness, or backseat barrier. Never let a pet ride in the front seat. For road trips over 4 hours, stop every 2–3 hours for toilet breaks, water, and exercise.

📱

Cab / App-Based Taxis

Ola and Uber do not have an official pet policy but rides are at the driver's discretion. Use Ola/Uber with the pet in a carrier and always inform the driver before confirming the ride. Some drivers may cancel.

🐾

Pet-Friendly Cab Services

Dedicated pet-friendly cab services are available in major cities (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi). Search "pet taxi" or "pet cab" in your city. These services have trained drivers and proper crates in the vehicle.

🗺️ Road Trip Safety Tips

Never leave a pet in a parked car
Car interior temperatures can rise to 60°C+ within minutes in Indian summers. This is life-threatening. Even with windows cracked, the heat builds up rapidly. This is also illegal under Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act.
Carry a pet first-aid kit
Include: bandages, antiseptic wipes, tweezers, oral rehydration salts, any regular medications, vet contact number, and nearest emergency vet along your route.
Water and no feeding 2 hours before
Avoid feeding pets 2 hours before the journey to reduce motion sickness. Keep water available. Do not let the pet drink large amounts just before moving.
Plan vet stops along your route
Research emergency vet clinics along the highway. Apps like VetsinPractice or PawBook can help locate 24-hour vets on your route.

🇮🇳 Exporting Pets from India — Procedure

1
Get ISO microchip implantedVisit a registered vet for ISO 11784/11785 15-digit microchip implantation. This must be done BEFORE the rabies vaccination for it to count toward international requirements.
2
Complete vaccination scheduleEnsure rabies, distemper, hepatitis, parvovirus, and leptospirosis are up to date. For some destinations, specific timelines apply (e.g., rabies at least 21–30 days before export).
3
Rabies titre test (if required by destination)Send blood sample to an FAVN/FAV-approved laboratory (OIE-accredited). India has approved labs in Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru. Allow 4–6 weeks for results.
4
Obtain International Health Certificate from DADFContact the Department of Animal Husbandry & Dairying (DADF). Their regional offices issue government-endorsed export health certificates. Location: Krishi Bhawan, New Delhi. Contact: 011-23382939.
5
Apply for No Objection Certificate (NOC)Some exotic pets (birds, reptiles) require NOC from MoEFCC (Ministry of Environment) under Wildlife Protection Act 1972. Apply online at parivesh.nic.in.
6
Book airline + file export declarationNotify Customs of live animal export. Cargo exports must be declared at the Air Cargo Complex. Carry all original documents, vaccination history, and microchip certificate.
Medical Emergency Travel
What to do when your pet needs emergency medical transport — by flight, train, or road. How to get priority booking and what documents you need.

🚨 Pet Medical Emergency Protocol

If your pet requires urgent medical travel — to a specialist hospital in another city or country — follow these steps immediately. Speed and correct documentation are critical.

01
Call your vet immediately
Get a written medical emergency certificate stating the pet's condition and urgency of travel. This document unlocks priority handling with carriers.
02
Call the airline/railway cargo desk
Explain the medical emergency. Some airlines (Air India) offer expedited processing for medical pet transport with an emergency vet letter.
03
Contact a pet transport agency
Specialist pet transport agencies handle documentation, carrier preparation, and logistics on your behalf — critical in an emergency. Contact: Jetpets India, Fido Fly, Pet Express.
04
Prepare emergency document pack
Health certificate, vaccination record, ownership proof, medical emergency letter from vet, and your own ID. Keep all in a waterproof folder attached to the crate.

✈️ Emergency Air Transport for Pets

⚠️
No guaranteed "emergency lane" for pet air travel in India

Unlike human medical emergencies, there is no mandated priority system for pets on commercial airlines. However, a vet emergency letter often helps expedite cargo booking and check-in procedures.

📞

Air India Medical Cargo

Call Air India Cargo: 1800-180-1407. Explain the medical emergency and request same-day or next-flight booking. Bring a written vet emergency certificate to the cargo terminal. Fees are standard — no emergency discount.

🚁

Chartered Pet Air Ambulance

For true life-threatening emergencies, pet air ambulances are available in India. Contact Jetpets India (+91-80-4699-8888) or Air Vets India. These services provide a climate-controlled, medically supervised flight with trained handlers.

🌍

International Emergency Transport

For international medical emergencies, IPATA (International Pet and Animal Transportation Association) members can arrange emergency pet transport within 24–48 hours. Contact an IPATA-accredited agent immediately.

📋

Documents for Emergency Flight

Vet emergency certificate (dated same day), vaccination certificate, health certificate (even if slightly expired — explain emergency), ownership proof, and your government ID. Border/airport officials have discretion in genuine emergencies.

🚆 Emergency Train Transport

1
Contact Station Master directlyGo to the Station Master's office at your departure station. Explain the medical emergency and present the vet's emergency certificate. The Station Master can allow same-day pet loading even without prior booking in genuine emergencies.
2
Indian Railways Helpline: 139Call 139 to inquire about the next available train with a brake van/luggage van heading to your destination. Ask for the train number and parcel office contact.
3
Bring all documents to Parcel OfficeVaccination record, health certificate (or vet emergency letter if certificate has expired), ownership proof. Pay charges on the spot and get the consignment receipt.
4
Arrange for someone to receive the petIf you cannot travel on the same train, arrange for a trusted person with the consignment note to receive the pet at the destination. Provide them with the vet's contact details and the receiving hospital's address.

Laws, Policies & Regulations
Key legal frameworks governing pet travel in India and internationally. Know your rights and responsibilities as a pet owner.

🇮🇳 Key Indian Laws for Pet Travel

⚖️

Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960

Section 11 prohibits carrying animals in conditions causing unnecessary suffering. Carriers must be properly ventilated, sized, and maintained. Violations can result in fines and criminal liability for the carrier and owner.

🌿

Wildlife Protection Act, 1972

Prohibits transport of any Schedule I-IV wildlife (including certain birds and reptiles) without a CITES permit. Applies to international travel and cross-state transport of protected species. Violations carry imprisonment up to 7 years.

🐾

Animal Quarantine & Certification Services (AQCS)

Under DADF, AQCS stations at major airports (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad) handle import/export health certification. Pets entering India are inspected at AQCS before customs clearance.

📋

DGCA Civil Aviation Requirements

DGCA Section 8, Series C regulates carriage of live animals by air in India. Airlines must follow IATA Live Animals Regulations (LAR). Airlines cannot refuse to carry pets if IATA requirements are met, subject to space availability.

🚆

Indian Railways Parcel Rules

Indian Railways Parcel Management System allows carriage of live animals as luggage in brake vans. The Railways Act 1989 governs liability for animals in transit. Railways are liable for negligence but not for natural death or escape.

🌐

Foreign Trade Policy — Exotic Pets

Export of certain exotic species (parrots, owls, mongoose, certain turtles) is prohibited under Schedule IV of WPA. Import of dogs/cats is governed by AQCS import rules. All imported pets must undergo health certification at the port of entry.

✅ Your Rights as a Pet Owner While Travelling

Right to claim compensation for injury/death in transit
If your pet is injured or dies due to carrier negligence, you may file a complaint with the Consumer Forum or DGCA (for airlines). Keep all receipts, documents, and photos of your pet before boarding.
Right to inspect cargo area conditions (request)
You can request to see the cargo area conditions before loading. Airlines are not legally obligated to allow this but many will accommodate the request.
Right to refuse loading if conditions are unsafe
If the cargo area is excessively hot, lacks ventilation, or the crate is damaged, you have the right to refuse loading and request a refund or rebooking.
File complaints with DGCA (airlines) or Ministry of Railways
Airline complaints: complaints.dgca@nic.in | Indian Railways complaints: pgportal.gov.in | PCA complaints: awbi.gov.in (Animal Welfare Board of India)
