Seller rules

  • Sell only genuine products, described honestly, with real photos and correct prices.
  • Ship on time, keep stock up to date, and answer customer questions politely.
  • Food, treats and medicines must be within their expiry date and stored properly.
  • No live animals, no prescription-only medicines, and nothing banned or harmful to animals.
  • No fake reviews or misleading offers. PawBook can pause or suspend stores that break these rules.
