🩺 Vet Camp Details
⚕️ Vet Verification Required: The supervising vet must have an active PawBook Vet registration. All participating vets must register with PawBook before the event. Pet owners must carry full medical history and their PawBook registration number.
Each pet owner will receive a printed + digital prescription after checkup. Any diagnosis must include clinical reasons. Organizer must upload all records to PawBook database before event closure.
🏠 Adoption Drive Details
📋 Registration Mandatory: Every attendee must pre-register and carry their Registration Number. Walk-ins without registration are not permitted to adopt.
🏆 Pet Show Details
🎖️ Registration Required: All participants must be pre-registered. No unregistered pet owners may participate in shows, catwalks, or competitions.
🎓 Training Event Details
🏫 Licensed Centers Only: Training centers must hold a valid license and be registered with PawBook. All trainers must be certified. Contact info and owner name required.
🌳 Meetup tips: Choose a safe public location (park, garden, beach, café). Mention if on/off leash, breed restrictions, or if food/water will be provided. All participants encouraged to register in advance.
