Welcome to PawBook ("App"), a product of Deemona Technologies ("we", "us", or "our"), based in New Delhi, India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and website at pawbook.deemona.com.

By using PawBook, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password when you register.
  • Pet Profile Information: Pet name, breed, age, photos, and other details you choose to share.
  • User Content: Posts, photos, comments, and messages you create on the platform.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version for troubleshooting.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, and time spent on the app.
  • Location Data: Only if you use the Vet Finder or Lost Pet features (with your permission).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the PawBook platform
  • Create and manage your account and pet profiles
  • Enable social features such as posts, comments, and follows
  • Send important notifications about your account or activity
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With your consent: When you choose to make your profile or posts public.
  • Service providers: Trusted third-party services (e.g., cloud hosting, database) that help us operate the platform, bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on servers hosted by Neon (PostgreSQL) and MilesWeb. We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and secure authentication using JSON Web Tokens (JWT).

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at support@deemona.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

PawBook is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it immediately.
