Emergency Response Guide

🚨 Pet Emergency Protocol
Follow these steps calmly. Bruno's health data has been pre-loaded for your vet.
Immediate Steps:
1
Stay Calm & Assess
Keep Bruno still and comfortable. Check breathing, consciousness, and visible injuries. Do not move a pet with spinal injury.
2
Call Ahead to the Vet
Alert your vet before arriving so they can prepare. Share Bruno's symptoms and your ETA. Use the contacts in the sidebar.
3
Share Bruno's Health Passport
Your vet can scan the QR code or use the link to access Bruno's full history instantly — vaccines, medications, allergies, blood type.
4
Transport Safely
Use a flat surface or stretcher for large dogs. Keep the pet warm. For small pets, use a carrier with ventilation. Drive safely — do not speed.
5
At the Hospital
Hand your phone to staff showing the health passport. Mention current medications: Heartgard Plus + NexGard + Omega-3. No known allergies.
