How to Report Effectively
1
Document safely — Take photos/videos from a safe distance. Don't confront the abuser.
2
Be specific — Include exact location, time, description of perpetrator if visible.
3
Note witnesses — Add any witness contact info to strengthen the case.
4
Your identity is protected — All reports are confidential by default.
5
Emergency? — Toggle the emergency flag to alert authorities immediately.
Legal Framework
Animal cruelty is punishable under Section 11 of the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960 and Section 428/429 of IPC. Offenders face fines up to ₹25,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years.
