The law is on their side
Cruelty to animals is an offence under the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960. Killing, poisoning or maiming an animal is punishable under Section 325 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (earlier IPC Sections 428/429) with up to 5 years' imprisonment, a fine, or both.
