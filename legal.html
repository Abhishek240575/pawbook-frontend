Usage Policy

Effective: 1 Oct 2026 · Last updated: 22 Oct 2026

This policy defines what is and is not acceptable on PawBook. Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or permanent ban.
1. Content Standards

All content on PawBook must be related to pets, animal welfare, or community well-being. We are a pet-first platform — keep it relevant, kind, and constructive.

Allowed

  • Pet photos, videos, stories, and milestones
  • Honest reviews of vets, shelters, and pet products
  • Educational content about pet health, training, and care
  • Lost & found pet alerts and adoption listings
  • Community events, meetups, and fundraisers for animal welfare

Not Allowed

  • Content unrelated to pets or the PawBook community
  • Graphic violence, gore, or disturbing imagery
  • Sexually explicit content of any kind
  • Spam, repetitive posts, or irrelevant promotions
  • Misinformation about pet health, breeds, or veterinary treatments
2. Animal Welfare

PawBook has a zero-tolerance policy for content that involves or promotes:

  • Animal cruelty, abuse, neglect, or harm in any form
  • Illegal wildlife trade or possession of exotic/prohibited animals
  • Dog fighting, cockfighting, or any animal combat
  • Sale of animals through channels that bypass welfare checks
  • Use of harmful training methods or devices

Such content will be removed immediately and reported to relevant authorities.

3. Community Behaviour
  • Be respectful — treat other pet owners and vets with kindness.
  • No harassment — do not bully, intimidate, or target other users.
  • No hate speech — content discriminating by religion, caste, gender, or ethnicity is prohibited.
  • No impersonation — do not pretend to be another person, vet, or organisation.
  • No doxxing — do not share private information about others without consent.
4. Commercial Use
  • Businesses may use PawBook to promote pet-related products and services within community guidelines.
  • Spam-style promotions, mass direct messages, or fake reviews are prohibited.
  • Multi-level marketing (MLM) schemes are not permitted.
  • All paid promotions must be clearly labelled as such.
5. Marketplace Rules
  • Only list items that are legal, genuine, and related to pets.
  • No listing of animals for sale unless through verified shelters or registered breeders.
  • Accurate descriptions and fair pricing are required.
  • Fraudulent listings will result in immediate account termination.
6. Health & Medical Claims
  • Do not make unverified medical claims about treatments, supplements, or cures.
  • Always recommend consulting a licensed veterinarian for health decisions.
  • Promotion of unapproved or unlicensed veterinary products is prohibited.
7. Account Security & Integrity
  • Creating multiple accounts to evade a suspension is prohibited.
  • Automated bots or scripts that interact with PawBook without permission are banned.
  • Selling, transferring, or purchasing PawBook accounts is not allowed.
  • Using VPNs to bypass regional restrictions may result in account review.
8. Enforcement

When a violation is detected, we may take one or more of the following actions depending on severity:

  • Remove the offending content
  • Issue a warning to the account holder
  • Temporarily suspend the account (1–30 days)
  • Permanently ban the account
  • Report to law enforcement where legally required

You may appeal enforcement actions by contacting appeals@deemona.com within 14 days.

9. Reporting Violations

Use the "Report" button on any post, comment, or profile to flag a violation. Alternatively, email safety@deemona.com with details and screenshots. All reports are reviewed within 48 hours.

Child Safety Policy

Effective: 1 Oct 2026 · Last updated: 22 Oct 2026

PawBook has a zero-tolerance policy for any content or behaviour that exploits, harms, or endangers children. This policy applies to all users, content, and interactions on the platform.
1. Prohibited Content

The following content is strictly prohibited on PawBook and will result in immediate account termination and reporting to law enforcement:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) of any kind — photos, videos, illustrations, or text
  • Content that sexualises, exploits, or endangers minors in any way
  • Grooming behaviour — attempts to build inappropriate relationships with minors online
  • Sharing personal information of minors without parental consent
  • Content that encourages, facilitates, or glorifies harm to children
  • Using PawBook to arrange meetings with minors for harmful purposes
2. Age Requirements

PawBook is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to create accounts.

  • Users aged 13–17 must have parental or guardian consent to use PawBook.
  • Parents or guardians who discover their child has created an account without consent should contact us immediately at safety@deemona.com.
  • We will promptly remove accounts identified as belonging to children under 13.
3. Our Detection & Prevention Measures
  • All uploaded media is subject to automated screening and human review for prohibited content.
  • We use hash-matching technology to detect and block known CSAM.
  • Accounts showing signs of targeting minors are flagged for immediate review.
  • We actively monitor for grooming patterns in messages and public content.
  • Direct messages are subject to review when abuse is reported.
4. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

If you encounter any content or behaviour that you believe endangers a child:

  • Use the Report button on the post, comment, or profile — select "Child Safety".
  • Email childsafety@deemona.com with details and any evidence.
  • All child safety reports are treated as the highest priority and reviewed within 2 hours.

For immediate danger to a child, contact local law enforcement first. In India, call 1098 (Childline) or 112 (Emergency).

5. Law Enforcement Cooperation

Deemona Global Solutions will cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies, including:

  • Providing account data, IP addresses, and content to authorities upon receipt of a valid legal request.
  • Proactively reporting CSAM to the National Centre for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and the Cyber Crime Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) as required.
  • Preserving evidence related to child safety investigations.
  • Responding to emergency law enforcement requests without delay.
6. Account Actions

Any account found to have violated this policy will face:

  • Immediate and permanent termination — no warnings, no appeals
  • Preservation of all relevant data for law enforcement
  • Reporting to Indian Cyber Crime authorities and NCMEC
  • IP-level blocking to prevent re-registration
7. Parental Guidance

We encourage parents and guardians to:

  • Monitor their children's use of social platforms, including PawBook.
  • Teach children not to share personal information online.
  • Report any inappropriate contact or content immediately.
  • Use parental control tools available on your device's operating system.
