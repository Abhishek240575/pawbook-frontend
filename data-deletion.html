Delete your PawBook data

You can ask us to delete your PawBook account and the personal data we hold about you at any time — including data we received when you signed in with Facebook or Google.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@deemona.com from the email address on your PawBook account.
  2. Use the subject “Delete my PawBook account”. If you signed in with Facebook and don't know which email we have, include your PawBook username or the name on your Facebook profile.
  3. We may reply to confirm the request comes from you before deleting anything.
Email a deletion request

We delete your personal data within 30 days of confirming your request, except records we must keep by law (for example, payment and order records under Indian tax law).

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile (name, username, email, phone, profile photo).
  • Your pets' profiles, health and vaccination records, posts, photos, comments and messages.
  • Your Facebook or Google sign-in link — the account ID and profile details we received from them.

If you signed in with Facebook

When you use “Continue with Facebook”, PawBook receives only your name, email address, profile photo and Facebook user ID. We never post to Facebook for you.

You can also stop PawBook's access from Facebook itself: on Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find PawBook and choose Remove. This stops new data being shared; to delete what we already hold, send the email above.
